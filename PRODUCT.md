# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Property Owners (primary, ~30–40):** Short-term rental hosts managing 1–5 properties. They check in on bookings and cleaning status throughout the day — often from their phone while on-site at a property. They need fast orientation, not dense configuration. Their primary task on any screen is "what needs my attention right now."

**Business Admin (secondary, 1):** The operations manager for the cleaning service. Works from a desktop in an office or home context, managing cleaners, reviewing all bookings across all owners, and handling scheduling conflicts. Their primary task is operational dispatch — knowing what's happening across the whole system.

## Product Purpose

Claro is a multi-tenant property cleaning scheduler. It connects short-term rental owners with the cleaning operations that happen between guest stays. Core product: guest-stay booking management plus cleaning task coordination. Success looks like: owners never miss a cleaning window, admins always know who is cleaning what and when.

## Brand Personality

Sophisticated, modular, premium. The tone is "high-end digital craftsmanship." It feels like a physical object — layers of frosted glass, soft lighting, and modular blocks that fit together perfectly.

Reference feel: **Linear** or **Apple.com** — modularity, depth, strong typographic hierarchy, and a focus on modular "Bento" units.

## Anti-references

- **Stark Admin Panels**: No flat, 2px-radius controls, no dense Bootstrap grids, no cold industrial aesthetics.
- **Over-animated dashboards**: Still avoid pulsing gradients and enterprise excess, but lean into subtle "spring" interactions.

## Design Principles

1. **The Bento Box.** Every feature or data point is housed in its own highly rounded (24px) container.
2. **Frosted Depth.** Use `backdrop-filter` and semi-transparent surfaces to create layers.
3. **Modular Rhythm.** Spacing between Bento blocks is consistent, creating a breathable, balanced grid.
4. **Lighting & Precision.** Use 1px subtle borders instead of heavy shadows to define edges.
5. **Context-aware density.** Admins see more Bento blocks; owners see fewer, larger blocks. The modularity scales naturally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No hard WCAG target specified. Follow sensible defaults: adequate contrast, keyboard-navigable interactive elements, clear focus indicators. Touch targets sized for mobile use (owners on-site at properties).
